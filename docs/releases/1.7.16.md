## Clipboard Search

- Optional local semantic search finds related saved text and images alongside immediate literal and OCR matches. Related Results preserve type filters, pinned entries and the selected item as results arrive.
- Enable Search Clipboard by Meaning in Clipboard settings on Apple silicon. After confirmation, Spotter downloads approximately 865 MB of checksum-verified model files from Hugging Face; clipboard content and queries remain on this Mac. Retry and verified local model import are available if setup fails.
- Indexing covers up to 10,000 recent retained entries. It does not open file-reference contents or scan folders. Clearing history removes derived vectors; turning semantic search off cancels work and deletes model files and vectors while preserving clipboard history. Consent and derived files do not sync.

Ordinary clipboard search remains available with semantic search off or unavailable. Quick Clipboard History keeps its existing grid.

Update through Check for Updates in Spotter, or download the DMG and drag Spotter to Applications.
