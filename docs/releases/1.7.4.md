# Spotter 1.7.4

## Quick AI Chat history
- Replaced the history sidebar with up to ten recent conversation capsules stacked above the input. Select a capsule to open the conversation at the same width, expanding upward.
- History uses a clock icon and stays on the right in both compact and expanded chat.
- History capsules use a solid background while the bottom input retains its gradient. Compact controls match the input’s native glass appearance.
- Smoothed the transition from a selected capsule into its conversation, with a reduced-motion alternative.
- Switching conversations continues to preserve each session’s draft and attachments.
