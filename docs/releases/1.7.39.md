## New

- **Switch Notes with a sideways swipe.** A horizontal scroll over the Note editor now selects the Note next to the one you are reading — one finger on a Magic Mouse, two on a trackpad. The page follows your finger as it does in Safari, so swiping left goes to the next Note and swiping right to the previous one, in the same order as the Emoji markers along the top, wrapping at either end just as ⌘[ and ⌘] do.

  Each swipe moves one Note, so a quick flick cannot skip several. Scrolling a long Note is untouched: a gesture that starts out vertical stays a scroll however much it drifts sideways, a swipe over the marker strip still scrolls the strip, and an ordinary scroll wheel behaves exactly as before.

Updating from 1.7.37 or 1.7.38 installs normally through Check for Updates. If you are still on 1.7.36 or earlier, install this version from the DMG once and grant Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Screen Recording and Full Disk Access again, as described in the 1.7.37 notes.
