## Fixes

- **Quick Clipboard History shows image thumbnails again.** Image entries could turn into grey placeholders and stay that way for as long as the menu was open. The menu now keeps the thumbnails it is showing and reloads any that go missing. Nothing in your history was affected; the images were always saved.

## Changes

- **Quick AI Chat is always dark.** The ⌥Space window used to contrast with the system, black in Light Mode and white in Dark Mode. It is now the black surface with light text in both, for the compact composer and the expanded conversation alike.

Updating from 1.7.37 installs normally through Check for Updates. If you are still on 1.7.36 or earlier, install this version from the DMG once and grant Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Screen Recording and Full Disk Access again, as described in the 1.7.37 notes.
