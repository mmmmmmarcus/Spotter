## Fixes

- Fixed feet-and-inches conversion results that displayed twelve inches instead of carrying into the next foot. For example, `2.1336m` now displays and copies as `7 feet 0 inches` rather than `6 feet 12 inches`. Negative lengths and the one-foot boundary are handled consistently, while ordinary fractional inches keep their existing precision.

Update through Check for Updates in Spotter, or download the DMG and drag Spotter to Applications.
