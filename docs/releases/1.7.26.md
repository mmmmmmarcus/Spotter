## A clearer AI Command editor

- Name and icon now share one row, with a visual picker offering 24 icons and a validated custom SF Symbol option.
- The prompt has a dedicated editing area with an empty-state hint. Selection guidance and Insert Selection sit together in its bottom toolbar.
- Model and shortcut controls align on separate rows, while Reset Prompt, Cancel and Save share a consistent footer.
- New commands focus the name field; existing commands focus the prompt. Existing commands, prompts, models and shortcuts are preserved.

Update through Check for Updates in Spotter, or download the DMG and drag Spotter to Applications.
