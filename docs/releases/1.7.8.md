# Spotter 1.7.8

## Quick AI Chat

- Open conversation history with the Up arrow from the compact input. Use Up and Down to select a conversation and Return to open it.
- Browse all previous conversations through the scrolling capsule list. Opening history from an expanded chat keeps the current conversation as a capsule above the input.
- Make keyboard selection clearer with a contrasting background, a brighter, thicker outline, and a short color transition.
- Simplify ordinary conversation capsules by removing their icons; AI Commands retain their command symbols.
- Start a fresh conversation when reopening Quick AI Chat more than one minute after closing it. Previous conversations and their drafts remain available in history.
- Correct user message bubbles to white with dark text in Light Mode and black with light text in Dark Mode, including command symbols and attachment labels.
