## Performance

- Reduce repeated query processing and unnecessary fuzzy matching while ranking launcher results. Search ranking and matching behavior are preserved.

## Screenshot fixes

- Keep the screen frozen during selection so transient menus and other selected content match the pixels copied. Region capture, whole-display capture, OCR and color picking use the same saved screen image; window capture retains its separate path.
- Prevent the screenshot crosshair from flickering as underlying applications update their cursors, and preserve window shadows in the frozen screen.
- Improve mixed Simplified Chinese and English text recognition. OCR now prioritizes these two languages; other scripts are no longer explicitly recognized, and large selections can take longer to recognize.
